MY JOURNEY IN PROGRAMMING

Big Bang Boom

Here lies the beginning of it all, the start of a new beginning, and the start of a new ending. HELLO WORLD! First is the question, "BA'T ANG BAGAL NANAMAN NG LAPTOP KO?!" I started wondering how. From discovering, learning, and acknowledging each part of the reason why, how, and what, I eventually found the reason why it was slow and also found another reason why I loved learning about it so much. Yes, it started with a slow laptop, a big imagination, and a big dream to conquer the world. The spark that lit my heart in pursuing technology began with a tiny problem that I eventually found a solution to. Now, I'm still growing, and I will grow much more.

Motivation

I started this journey with a single spark, but as I went on with it, that single spark exploded, expanded, and grew even more. From a little spark, I found many more things that I wanted to pursue. Those things motivated me and still motivate me now. I also found the discipline and knowledge I need to move forward. My motivation? To do it all for one person: "Just Doing It, For Her."

Journey

The journey so far has been such a pain, but the experiences and new things I have learned have been something new and exciting. The journey was fun, complex, weird, and full of questions: "Ito ba talaga ang course na para sa akin?" It kept bothering me along the way, but I used the question as a way to push myself further. "Ito nga pala talaga ang para sa akin." That saying pushed me forward. I went from zero, and now I'm farther than where I was, but still far from where I want to be. The journey ahead will be more, and I expect it to be more fun, complex, and challenging. I will take them all, one line at a time.
